Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, improve site performance, and understand how visitors use the website.

CarnegienFreedom uses cookies and similar technologies only to support the operation of our website and to improve the user experience where appropriate.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They help keep pages secure, support navigation, and enable core features such as access to forms or protected content.

These cookies do not require your consent because they are necessary for the service to operate.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website, such as which pages are visited most often or whether visitors encounter errors. This helps us improve the site and the content we provide.

Analytics cookies are used only where you have expressly consented to them or where they are otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies may remember preferences such as language, accessibility settings, or other choices so that your experience is more consistent on future visits.

Marketing Cookies

We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies for targeted advertising. If we add such technologies in the future, we will update this Cookie Policy and seek the appropriate consent before enabling them.

3. Consent

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent banner asking you to choose your preferences. You can:

  • Accept all cookies;
  • Reject non-essential cookies; or
  • Customise your cookie preferences.

You can change your preferences at any time by revisiting the cookie settings on the homepage or by contacting us at hello@carnegienfreedom.com.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services to support the operation, security, or analysis of the Site. These providers may place cookies on your device subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not sell personal data for marketing purposes, and we do not currently use behavioural advertising cookies.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, or to alert you when a website attempts to place a cookie on your device.

Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

6. Data Retention

Cookie data is generally kept for the period needed to support the purposes described above. Some cookies are session-based and are deleted when you close your browser, while others may remain for a longer period depending on their purpose and your preferences.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our technology, legal obligations, or service delivery. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about our use of cookies or this Cookie Policy, please contact us:

Email: hello@carnegienfreedom.com

For more information about how we handle your personal data, please read our Privacy Policy.
